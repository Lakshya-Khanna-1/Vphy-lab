# VphyLAB

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
University physics students, for independent practice and scheduled college labs (confirmed by user).

## Product Purpose
Choose an experiment, manipulate simulated instruments, capture readings, compare theory and observations, and export results.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Preserve the five experiments: bar pendulum, optical fibre, Newton's rings, spectrometer, RC circuit. Preserve simulation hooks, Firebase integration, observation tables and CSV export. Existing Next.js 16, React 19, Tailwind 4 stack. Backend and experiment behavior stay intact during the visual redesign.

## Brand Commitments
VphyLAB; GTBIT institutional association. The user requests an unusual, distinctive design that students want to use daily.

## Evidence on Hand
Working simulations and existing Firebase functions in src. No testimonials or usage claims supplied.

## Product Principles
Keep the experiment within easy reach. Make instructions and readings legible. Distinguish real saved data from empty states. Support desktop and mobile navigation.
